Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Cash Flow X is a free business-tycoon game at cash-flow-x.avmenon76.workers.dev. This page explains what the game stores about you and why. We keep it to the minimum the game needs.

What we collect

You can play without signing in, and then nothing leaves your device. If you choose Sign in with Google, we receive from Google:

  • Your Google account ID (a random identifier), to recognise you when you come back.
  • Your name from your Google profile, used as your display name. You can see and change it in the game.

We do not receive or store your email address, contacts, photo, password, or any other Google data. Sign-in uses Google's standard Google Identity Services, and Google never gives us your password.

Game data we store

When you play online we store what the game needs to run and to verify the leaderboards:

  • your display name and chosen icon, company names, and your game progress (cash, scores, prestige, perks, gems, achievements);
  • the moves you make in a game, so the server can replay them and confirm leaderboard scores are genuine;
  • guild membership and holding-company or investment records, if you use those features;
  • a sign-in session token (stored only as a one-way hash on the server) so you stay signed in for up to 60 days.

What other players can see

Your display name, icon, company name and scores can appear on public leaderboards, and to guild members and other players when you use the social features. Guild members only see detail your visibility setting allows. Don't use a display name you would not want shown publicly.

On your device

The game saves your progress and preferences in your browser's local storage, and uses a service worker to let the game run offline. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not run analytics. The page loads fonts from Google Fonts, so Google may receive your IP address when you load the game.

Where it is stored and who processes it

Data is hosted on Cloudflare (Workers and D1 database). Cloudflare and Google act as service providers for hosting and sign-in. We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and game data until you ask us to delete it. Local data in your browser can be cleared at any time through your browser settings.

Your choices and rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the data we hold about you, including your whole account. Contact us using the details below and tell us your display name. You can also revoke the game's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above.

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/jonathansunil2358-maker/Cash-Flow-X.
